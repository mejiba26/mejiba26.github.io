## About

MSBA candidate at Wake Forest (May 2027), Finance and Economics background, targeting markets analyst and equity research roles.

I've been trading futures independently since 2020, which shaped how I think about companies: a business has an intrinsic value and a market price, and the gap between them is where the interesting questions live. My RTX valuation was the first time I built that view formally, and revisiting it in 2026 taught me as much as building it. I audited my original model, caught two errors, and rebuilt it. That's the kind of work I want to do full time: models built to be questioned.
