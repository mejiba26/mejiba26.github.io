03 — Work Evidence

Projects

Context, contribution, outcome — in that order.

Item 01

RTX Corporation Equity Valuation

Context

Independent equity research project to build a full valuation of a large-cap defense contractor.

My role

Built a DCF and CAPM-based valuation model from public filings, projecting free cash flow and estimating fair value per share.

Outcome

Produced a complete investment thesis with a defensible price target, applying the same framework used in professional equity research.

Item 02

Housing Price Regression Model

Context

Statistics assignment analyzing 545 home sales to identify what drives housing prices.

My role

Built a multiple regression model, tested and removed a statistically insignificant variable (bedrooms, p = 0.114), and interpreted the final 11-predictor model.

Outcome

Final model explains 68% of price variation (R² = 0.680); bathrooms and hot water heating emerged as the two strongest price drivers, adding roughly $1.02M and $862K respectively.

Item 03

Independent Futures Trading

Context

Self-directed trading of MNQ and MES futures contracts, ongoing since 2020.

My role

Developed a price-action approach, sizing every position at 2–5% account risk and journaling each trade for review.

Outcome

Five years of consistent, disciplined risk management — the same instinct that drives sound investment decision-making.

04 — Commercial Awareness

Market View

Three things I'm tracking right now, and why they matter to the roles I want.

Trend

Fed rate policy & equity valuations

The Fed has held its target range at 3.50–3.75% through 2026, with futures markets pricing the effective rate near 4% by year-end. Rate expectations move straight into every DCF I build — a shift in the discount rate changes what a company is actually worth, before the stock even moves.

Trend

Earnings season: valuation checkpoint & trading catalyst

The BLS projects 6% growth in financial analyst roles through 2034 — demand tracks how often companies need their numbers re-examined. On the trading side, earnings prints are some of the highest-volatility moments I watch in NQ and ES. Research and trading meet at the same moment: the numbers update my fundamental view, and the reaction is what I'm positioned for.

Trend

Sector rotation

Energy and financials have led the S&P 500 through mid-2026 while tech cooled on AI-valuation concerns — a real-time example of capital moving on rate and macro conditions. That rotation reshapes both which companies look attractive to own and which ones are moving enough to trade.
