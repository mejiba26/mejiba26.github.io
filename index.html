03 — Work Evidence

Projects

Project 01

RTX Corporation Equity Valuation

Fall 2024, revised 2026

Context

Independent equity research on a large cap defense contractor, built to answer one question: what is RTX actually worth per share?

My role

Built a DCF at a 6.15% WACC with sensitivity analysis, then cross checked it against peer P/E and EV/EBITDA multiples. Benchmarked RTX against Boeing and Lockheed Martin on profitability, liquidity, and leverage. RTX's 19.21% gross margin was the strongest in the peer set.

Outcome

Fair value of $125 to $133 per share vs. $122 at the time of analysis, so modestly undervalued.

What changed

Models built to be questioned, including my own. I used Claude to audit my original 2024 model, caught two errors, and rebuilt it. My estimate dropped from $175 to $128 per share, and I documented every change in a new appendix.

Project 02

ECG Patient Matching Model

Wake Forest School of Medicine Build-A-Thon · Sept 2026 to present

Context

A Build-A-Thon hosted by the Center for Artificial Intelligence Research (CAIR). The task: score whether two Lead I ECGs belong to the same patient.

My role

Led the modeling for a three person team. Built a Siamese CNN and feature neural net ensemble in Python (TensorFlow/Keras), trained on 37,000+ ECGs from 10,000 MIMIC-IV patients plus St. Jude paired hospital and Apple Watch ECGs.

Outcome Preliminary

Improved AUROC on held out patients from 0.948 (v1) to 0.984 (v3) for hospital vs. Apple Watch matching. Every setting was tuned on a separate practice set so the locked test stayed clean.

0.984 is our own held out test, not the official competition score. Final submission is October 13, 2026.
